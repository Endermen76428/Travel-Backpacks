# Travel-Backpacks
Travel Backpacks is a simple addon to help with your full inventory problem.
See below how to craft it and use:

# Craft

Using **1 chest, 4 leather and 4 strings** you can craft a **Leather Backpack** the first tier of others backpacks.

![Backpack Craft](doc/1.0%20-%20Craft%20Backpack.png)

With a Backpack in hands, you can **interact to open it**.
To place your Backpack on the ground you just need to **crouch and interact with a block**.

![Open Backpack](doc/1.1%20-%20Open%20Backpack.gif)

# Inventory UI

Each backpack has a custom UI that is compatible with other custom UIs.

![Backpack Inventory](doc/3.0%20-%20Backpack%20Inventory.gif)

If you are playing in a cell phone your UI will be changed, to use a scrolling panel.

![Backpack Inventory Mobile](doc/3.1%20-%20Backpack%20Inventory%20Mobile.png)

## Paint Backpack

Using a dye on your backpack on the ground you can paint it.

![Painting Backpack](doc/2.7%20-%20Paint%20Backpack.gif)
![Backpack Colors](doc/2.8%20-%20Backpack%20Colors.gif)

# Upgrade Template

To craft any upgrades you will need an **Upgrade Template**.

![Upgrade Template Craft](doc/2.0%20-%20Craft%20Template%20Upgrade.png)

# Upgrade Tiers

To increase the storage space of your backpacks you can craft **Upgrades Tier**.

![Copper Upgrade](doc/2.1%20-%20Craft%20Copper%20Upgrade.png)
![Iron Upgrade](doc/2.2%20-%20Craft%20Iron%20Upgrade.png)
![Gold Upgrade](doc/2.3%20-%20Craft%20Gold%20Upgrade.png)
![Diamond Upgrade](doc/2.4%20-%20Craft%20Diamond%20Upgrade.png)
![Netherite Upgrade](doc/2.5%20-%20Craft%20Netherite%20Upgrade.png)

Hold the upgrade on your hand and interact with the backpack to upgrade its tier.
TIP: You don't need to follow the tier progress, you can craft the last tier and apply it in a letter backpack.

![Upgrading Backpack](doc/2.6%20-%20Upgrade%20Backpack.gif)

# Craft Upgrade

The **Craft Upgrade** unlock an craft panel on your backpack that works as a vanilla crafting table. **(It's compatible with any addon's recipes)**.

![Craft Upgrade Craft](doc/4.0%20-%20Craft%20Craft%20Upgrade.png)

![Craft Terminal](doc/4.1%20-%20Craft%20Upgrade.gif)

# Furnace Upgrade

The **Furnace Upgrade** unlock an furnace panel on your backpack that works as a vanilla furnace. **(It's compatible with any addon's recipes)**.

![Furnace Upgrade Craft](doc/5.0%20-%20Craft%20Furnace%20Upgrade.png)

![Furnace Panel](doc/5.1%20-%20Furnace%20Upgrade.gif)
